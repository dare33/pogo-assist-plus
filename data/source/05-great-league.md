# Great League (1500 CP)

_As of 24 Sep 2026, Season 28 "Twilight Trails". Ranks and scores are PvPoke's overall
rankings (data dated 23 Sep 2026). IVs are the rank-1 spread written Atk/Def/HP @ level._

## What matters in this mode

- **Low Attack, high Defence and HP.** Maximum stat product under 1500 CP is the goal, so the ideal IVs are usually 0–6 Attack with 13–15 in the rest. Check spreads with PvPoke's IV checker or Poke Genie. Rank 100 or better is within about 1% of rank 1; do not overpay for rank 1.
- **Elite TMs are the scarce resource.** This season's Battle Research hands out one Elite Fast and one Elite Charged TM per page. Spend them on Melmetal, Ninetales, Altaria, Quagsire and Florges first.
- **Levels above 40 need XL candy** (296 XL from 41 to 50). Only a few Great League rank-1 spreads sit above 40 (Galarian Corsola 47.5, Shadow Sableye 47).
- **Best Buddy adds one level.** Re-check the spread with the boost toggled; it can push you over the cap.
- **Second charged move** costs 10k to 100k dust by species, 1.2× for Shadows. Every entry below wants two.
- **Shadow vs regular is per species.** Shadow wins for Ninetales, Quagsire, Sableye; regular wins for Altaria and Corviknight here.
