# Ultra League (2500 CP)

_As of 24 Sep 2026, Season 28 "Twilight Trails". PvPoke overall rankings, data dated 23 Sep
2026. IVs are the rank-1 spread written Atk/Def/HP @ level._

## What matters in this mode

- **Most of the top 10 needs XL candy.** Tinkaton, Corviknight, Mimikyu, Jellicent, Dusknoir and Registeel all want level 47 to 50. Budget 296 XL per build.
- **Non-XL top picks exist:** Shadow Snorlax (level 28.5), Melmetal (25.5), Virizion (31), Zygarde (24), Empoleon (33), Florges (28.5), Feraligatr (35), Guzzlord (34).
- **Same IV logic as Great League** (low Attack, high bulk) except where the rank-1 spread is near-hundo at level 50 (Tinkaton 12/15/15, Cramorant 15/15/15, Mimikyu 13/15/15).
- **Ultra League Mega Edition** runs on alternate weeks this season with Megas legal at reduced CP.
