# Gym defence

_As of 24 Sep 2026. A shallow, solved meta. Defence is mostly about coin timing, not winning._

## What matters in this mode

- **Coins:** 1 per 10 minutes, capped at 50 per day, paid only when your defender is knocked out. A defender that sits for three days still pays 50. Aim to be knocked out after about 8 h 20 m in a gym that turns over daily.
- **Motivation decay scales with max CP.** A 3,000+ CP defender craters in hours; a sub-1,500 CP Chansey or Wobbuffet decays slowly and needs no berries.
- **The defender AI fires moves on a timer**, so slow, hard-hitting fast moves and multi-bar charged moves are fine. STAB and coverage against Fighting (what attackers bring) matter more than DPS.
- **Never stack Normal types.** One Machamp sweeps the whole gym. Mix Normal with Fairy, Ghost, Flying or Psychic so attackers must switch.

## Practical setup

- Place a low-CP Chansey or Wobbuffet in a gym you pass daily; it will still be there tomorrow.
- Feed berries only when you are within an hour of the 50-coin mark and want it to hold.
- Nothing released in 2026 changed this list.
