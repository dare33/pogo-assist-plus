# High-tier raids — the priority roster

_As of 24 Sep 2026. The ~20 Pokémon a heavy raider should power up for Tier 5, Mega, Shadow,
Elite and Super Mega raids. Ordered by how often you will actually use them._

## What matters in this mode

- **Breadth across types** beats one perfect team. Bosses rotate weekly.
- **Bulk matters more at T5** than in T3 solos. Shadows and glass cannons need relobbies; bulky legendaries and Megas stay in.
- **Super Mega Raids** (since Feb 2026) need 7+ trainers and shield-breaking Megas. Primals cannot break shields; Mega Mewtwo X's Dynamic Punch+ breaks two.
- **Fusions** (Kyurem, Necrozma) cost 1,000 Fusion Energy plus 30 candy of each component. Energy comes only from the relevant raids, so farm them when they rotate in.

## Mega Energy priority

Worth energy: Rayquaza, Mewtwo Y/X, Delphox, Raichu Y, Lucario, Garchomp, Gengar, Tyranitar, Metagross, Gardevoir, Diancie, Starmie, Victreebel, Dragonite.
Not worth it: Mega Malamar, Mega Falinks.
