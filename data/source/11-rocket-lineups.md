Where a slot has several options they are separated by " / ": the leader brings one of them. Names are written the way the game master resolves them; `npm run build:hub` fails on any it cannot resolve. "Since" is a date (`yyyy-mm-dd`) or a month (`Oct 2026`) when the day is not published: no source gives the start of the current takeover, so all four are "Oct 2026". ## Grunt quotes

What a grunt says before the battle tells you the type of its team. The two typeless rows have an empty Type and a Label: the generic grunt (male or female) of a mixed team, and the Decoy.
