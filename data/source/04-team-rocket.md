# Team GO Rocket battles

_As of 24 Sep 2026. Lineups rotated on 25 Jun 2026 and rotate again on 2 Oct 2026 (Harvest
Festival: Taken Over). The attacker tier below outlives any rotation._

## What matters in this mode

- **Energy and cheap charged moves beat raw DPS.** Grunts never shield and pause after your charged move or a switch. Leaders and Giovanni shield your first two charged moves, so bait with a 35-energy move and save the nuke for the third.
- **Fast fast moves** (Counter, Mud Shot, Powder Snow, Psycho Cut) generate energy quickest. Build around them.
- **Giovanni needs a Super Rocket Radar.** Since April 2026 those come only from "Taken Over" GO Pass tracks, and Giovanni appears only at PokéStops during those events.
- **Frustration** (the move every Rocket Shadow has) can only be TM'd off during Taken Over events. Next: 2–5 Oct 2026.

## Which Shadows to keep

- **Default: keep Shadow.** Purify only for a PvP IV spread or a cheap power-up on something you will never raid with.
- **Keep every Shadow legendary** in Shadow form: Shadow Reshiram now, Shadow Zekrom from 2 Oct 2026, Shadow Thundurus and Shadow Landorus from Shadow raids.
- **Top Stardust priorities from Rocket:** Shadow Mewtwo, Shadow Garchomp, Shadow Machamp.
- **Worth catching from the current rotation for PVE:** Shadow Tyranitar and Shadow Camerupt (Cliff), Shadow Scizor, Alakazam and Charizard (Arlo), Shadow Rhyperior and Machamp (Giovanni decoys), Shadow Snorlax and Lapras (typeless grunt), Shadow Houndoom, Blastoise and Steelix (Sierra).
- **PvP-relevant in the current pool:** Shadow Machamp, Shadow Alakazam, Shadow Steelix, Shadow Snorlax (#3 Ultra League), Shadow Charizard.
- A Shadow with Frustration cannot receive a Community Day move on evolution. TM Frustration first, then evolve in the CD window.

## Mechanics

- Rocket Radar = 6 Mysterious Components (one per grunt win). Balloons roughly every 6 hours.
- **Shadow raids:** in person only, T1/T3 daily, T5 on weekends. The boss enrages mid-fight; the lobby needs 8 Purified Gems (4 Shadow Shards each, from grunts and leaders) to subdue it. Sep 2026: Shadow Thundurus Incarnate weekends until 6 Oct; Oct adds Shadow Landorus Incarnate. T1/T3 pool: Shadow Machop, Bellsprout, Torchic, Bagon, Alolan Sandslash, Quagsire, Lampent.
- **Harvest Festival: Taken Over, 2–5 Oct 2026:** Shadow Zekrom debuts (shiny-eligible) via the GO Pass Super Rocket Radar; leaders and grunts rotate; Shadow Wimpod, Sandygast and Fomantis join; research gives TMs and Mysterious Components.
