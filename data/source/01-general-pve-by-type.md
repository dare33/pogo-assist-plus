# General PVE — best raid attackers by type

_As of 24 Sep 2026. In practice "best PVE Pokémon" means "best counter of each type", because
you pick attackers against the boss's weaknesses. Top 4 per type plus a budget line. See
`02-high-tier-raids.md` for the cross-type priority list._

## What matters in this mode

- **Moveset beats IVs.** A 3-star with the right charged move out-damages a hundo with the wrong one. IVs move damage by under 1% in most raids.
- **Shadow versions usually win** on damage (+20% dealt, +20% taken). Frustration can only be removed during Rocket "Taken Over" events. Next window: 2–5 Oct 2026.
- **Megas boost the whole lobby** (10% damage, 30% same-type) and give bonus candy. One per lobby, so agree who brings it.
- **Level 40 breadth first, level 50 depth second.** Build six good counters at 40 before pushing any to 50.
- **Obtain difficulty** is rated for a heavy player who buys passes. Easy / Medium / Hard / Locked as defined in `README.md`.
