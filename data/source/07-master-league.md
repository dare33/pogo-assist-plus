# Master League (no CP cap)

_As of 24 Sep 2026, Season 28 "Twilight Trails". PvPoke overall rankings, data dated 23 Sep
2026. Master League weeks this season: 29 Sep–6 Oct, 27 Oct–3 Nov, 24 Nov–1 Dec. The limited
format is Master League Mega Edition; there is no Premier or Classic this season._

## What matters in this mode

- **Everything wants 15/15/15 at level 50, then Best Buddy for 51.** A 14 Attack hundo loses charged-move priority ties to a 15. Legendaries need about 296 XL plus raid candy.
- **The cheap builds are the non-legendaries:** Metagross (#4), Ursaluna (#10), Garchomp, Melmetal. Start there.
- **Legacy moves gate most of the top 10.** Spacial Rend, Behemoth Blade, Roar of Time, Ice Burn and Freeze Shock come from specific events or fusions and cannot be Elite TM'd outside their windows.
- **Best Buddy is a free 2% stat gain here** with no CP-cap downside. Worth 300 hearts on Zacian, Palkia and Zygarde.
