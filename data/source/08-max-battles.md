# Max Battles (Dynamax and Gigantamax)

_As of 24 Sep 2026. A separate mode with its own roster, fought in person at Power Spots.
Tiers follow Pokémon GO Hub's Max attacker, defender and healer lists._

## What matters in this mode

- **Max Particles (MP) are the binding constraint.** About 100–120 per Power Spot tap, 300 per 2 km walked, collection capped at 800 per day (resets 05:00), storage capped at 1,500. A 6-star Gigantamax battle costs 800 MP, so that is one attempt per day at base rates. Save MP for Max Battle Days, when spots pay 8× and the cap rises to 1,600.
- **Fast attacks fill the Max meter; 0.5-second fast moves (Pound, Lick) fill it twice as fast.** That is why Blissey and Zamazenta are the tanks.
- **Max Moves level separately** from normal moves: L1 400 MP + 50 candy, L2 600 MP + 100 candy, L3 800 MP + 40 XL. Candy scales by species cost group; legendaries cost far more. Level Max Attack on your attackers, Max Guard on one or two tanks, Max Spirit on Blissey.
- **Use a Gigantamax if the boss is weak to it.** The G-Max move replaces Max Attack and is what makes them S tier. A-tier Dynamax picks are fine when you lack the G-Max.
- **Dynamax Pokémon work everywhere else too** (raids, gyms, Rocket, PvP). They just cannot Dynamax outside Max Battles. A high-IV Dynamax legendary is a dual-purpose build.
- **Standard team:** one tank (Blissey or Zamazenta, Max Guard L3), one healer (Blissey, Max Spirit), one or two type-matched attackers. Fast attacks only, and dodge the boss's charged moves before you Max.

**Eternatus** rates as the best raw Max attacker but is not in the active roster. Legacy unless you already have one.
